A continuous piecewise local-y mapping reduces the nominal middle visor band to 82% of its previous height and redistributes height into the chin, with the same mapping applied to shell, glass and plastic before the wireframe is built. This preserves adjoining mesh boundaries, avoiding the overlapping band and exposed blue shell seen when compressing only glass. Vertex normals are transformed for the vertical scale; the underlying GLB is not altered. The map is an approximation of presentation, not an engineering model or reproduction of a specific manufactured helmet.
