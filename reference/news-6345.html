Max previews Sao Paulo GP: 'Hope we can have another solid weekend'

Published on 04 November 2026 by Niels Hendrix

After winning in Mexico, next up is the Grand Prix of Sao Paulo. Max Verstappen, who will be racing with a special helmet in Brazil, said: “It was amazing to get the win in Mexico and huge credit goes to the team. The last few weekends have been a lot more promising and I didn’t think I would be winning three races this year. This is a hugely positive step in the right direction as we head to the final few races.”

Max continued: “I always look forward to going to Brazil. It is a fantastic place and is obviously pretty special as my family in law are there. The track has been good to me in the past and we have had a lot of great drives and good memories, so I hope we can have another solid weekend. I’ll be wearing my special helmet in Brazil, with the design to be something nice for the fans and to show what a special place this is for me. It’s a cool track to drive and I’m excited to be back here.”
